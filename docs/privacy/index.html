Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

heycam is a document scanner for Android. Your scans stay on your phone. heycam has no account, no ads and no servers, and it does not upload your documents anywhere.

Who makes heycam

heycam is made by Usham Roy, an individual developer in India. Questions about this policy: support@edgr.in.

What stays on your phone

  • Scanning. The camera and scanning screens come from Google ML Kit, through Google Play services. Google describes the document scanner as running entirely on the device. heycam does not ask for camera permission.
  • Photos and files. Images you import or share into heycam are copied into heycam's private storage. heycam saves them again without their metadata, such as location.
  • Text. Text in your scans is recognised on the phone by Google ML Kit. heycam stores it with the document so you can search, copy and export it.
  • Settings. Your choices, such as theme and export defaults, are stored on the phone.

What Google ML Kit sends to Google

heycam uses Google ML Kit and Google Play services. They send Google diagnostics and usage information about how ML Kit is working: device information (such as manufacturer, model and Android version), app information (such as package name and app version), device or per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API settings, input and output sizes, feature versions, event types and error codes. Google says this is sent over HTTPS and used for diagnostics and usage analytics. Images and recognised text are not on Google's list. Details: Google ML Kit data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

The scanner and some text-recognition models are downloaded once from Google Play services, which needs an internet connection the first time.

What heycam itself collects

Nothing. heycam has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own, and its own code makes no network requests. If you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may give the developer anonymous crash and performance reports (Android vitals).

When files leave your phone

Only when you choose: when you share, save, export or print a document, it goes to the app, folder or printer you pick. Shared files are handed over through Android's share sheet with access for that one app only.

The Tools tab links to heypdf.edgr.in, which opens in your browser. heycam sends nothing there. Files you upload to heypdf leave your phone and are processed on heypdf's server, under heypdf's own privacy policy.

Permissions

heycam asks you for no permissions. Its install includes internet and network-state access, which Google Play services and ML Kit use for the downloads and diagnostics described above.

How long data is kept

  • Documents stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall heycam.
  • Deleted documents wait in Trash for 30 days and are removed for good the next time heycam opens after that. You can empty Trash sooner.
  • Copies made for sharing go to a temporary folder. heycam deletes them the next time it opens after a day, or when you clear temporary files.
  • Uninstalling heycam removes all of its data from the phone.

Backups and new phones

heycam keeps your scans out of Google's cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, a direct phone-to-phone transfer can bring them to a new phone. If you lose your phone, any document you have not exported or transferred is lost with it.

Security

heycam keeps your documents in its private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's app sandbox and your phone's screen lock and storage encryption protect them. heycam does not add encryption of its own.

Your choices

You can delete any document, empty Trash and clear temporary files in heycam, or uninstall it to remove everything. heycam has no server, so there is no copy of your documents for us to delete. For data that Google holds, see Google's privacy policy.

Children

heycam is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Changes are posted on this page with a new date at the top. Every earlier version is kept in the public history of heycam's source repository.
