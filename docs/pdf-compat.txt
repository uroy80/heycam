heycam searchable PDF: viewer compatibility (gate G2, PLAN 6.5)
Owner: O5 (PDF & export). Update this file once per release candidate.

WHAT IS CHECKED
- The invisible text layer of heycam's PDFs: search, copy and selection alignment in real viewers.
- Writer: :core:pdf PdfBoxPdfService (PDFBox-Android 2.0.27.0). One Tj per OCR line, render mode 3, Type0 /
  Identity-H font with an embedded one-glyph TrueType (GlyphlessFont, generated in code), ToUnicode bfchar per
  CID, Tz so each line spans its box, line angle through Tm, header %PDF-1.7. A gently tilted line is turned
  towards horizontal about its centre until its baseline climbs or falls by at most 0.4 x its height (poppler cuts a
  line once that passes about half the height), provided its ends move by no more than 0.6 x its height; a steeper
  line keeps its own angle (TextLayer.level).

FIXTURES
- Made by a Robolectric test:
    ./gradlew :core:pdf:testDebugUnitTest --tests '*PdfCompatFixturesTest*'
  Output: core/pdf/build/pdf-fixtures/*.pdf plus expected.txt ("<file><TAB><text that must be found>").
- latin.pdf     FIT, no margin        Latin lines, an e-mail address
- hindi.pdf     A4, narrow margin     Devanagari and English
- cjk.pdf       Letter, normal margin Japanese, Korean (with a space), hiragana
- rotated.pdf   Legal, landscape page two lines turned 3 degrees
- tilted.pdf    A4, normal margin     three long lines turned 2.5 degrees (a scan that is not quite square)
- non-bmp.pdf   A4                    a character outside the Basic Multilingual Plane (U+20BB7)

AUTOMATED CHECKS (CI, ubuntu)
- for each fixture: qpdf --check <file>; pdftotext -enc UTF-8 <file> - | grep -F <expected text>
- The JVM tests already read every fixture back with PDFBox's PDFTextStripper and check glyph positions are within
  2 pt of the OCR line box (PdfBoxPdfServiceTest); a levelled line by design sits off its box by up to 0.6 x its
  height at its ends and on it at its centre (TextLayerTest).

How: PDFium, pdfminer, Ghostscript and pikepdf from Python in a container; pdf.js from Firefox's omni.ja
(chrome/pdfjs/content/build) under Node; PDFKit from a Swift script. Each reads expected.txt and looks for every
string in the engine's text (whitespace runs counted as one space) and, where the engine has one, with its search.
Tilt sweep (six lines, Latin, CJK and Devanagari, 24 to 48 px, turned 0.6, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 10 and 20 degrees;
54 strings): PDFium, PDFKit, pdf.js, pdfminer and Ghostscript find all 54 at every angle. poppler finds every line
up to 3 degrees. A line is levelled only while it is at most 1.6 / sin(angle) times as long as it is high (23 at
4 degrees, 15 at 6, 9 at 10); a longer one keeps its angle and poppler still cuts it. Before levelling, poppler cut
long lines from 1.5 degrees.

Fixed while running G2:
- Apple CoreGraphics ignored the whole text layer ("Failed to determine ascent and descent for font") while the
  font descriptor said Descent 0. It now says -1, as Tesseract's glyphless font does; a test guards it.
- With every character at half an em, Tz stretched spaces as much as letters; in short Latin or Korean lines the
  word gap grew wide enough for poppler to split one line into columns. CIDs now carry rough natural widths
  (W array): 1 em for CJK and full-width forms, 1/4 em for spaces, 1/2 em otherwise (DW 500). Tz is still
  100 x boxWidth / (advance x size); for plain half-em text that is PLAN 3.10's formula.
