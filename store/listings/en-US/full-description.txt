Point, scan, done. heycam turns paper into clean PDFs and reads the text in them, right on your phone.

SCAN
• Finds the edges of the page and straightens it
• Takes the picture by itself when the page is steady
• Many pages in one go, or pick photos from your gallery
• Removes stains and fingers while you scan
• Filters: Original, Auto, Grey and Black & white. Change them any time.

EDIT
• Rotate, reorder, add, retake or delete pages
• PDF pages in A4, Letter, Legal or fitted to the scan, with or without margins

READ THE TEXT
• Latin script (English, Spanish, French and more), Devanagari (Hindi, Marathi and more), Chinese, Japanese and Korean
• Searchable PDFs: find and copy words in your PDF reader
• Copy, fix and share the text
• Search your scans by name or by the words inside them

KEEP IT TIDY
• Folders, pins and recent scans
• A trash you can restore from

SHARE
• PDF, JPG, PNG or plain text
• Small, Standard or Original size, with the file size shown before you save
• Share, print, or save a copy to any folder, including cloud storage apps on your phone

YOURS
• Your scans stay on your phone. heycam never uploads them.
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no watermark
• Scan from Quick Settings or a long press on the app icon
• Dark mode, large text and TalkBack
• Open source under Apache-2.0: github.com/uroy80/heycam

GOOD TO KNOW
• Scanning needs Google Play services and about 2 GB of RAM. The scanner downloads once from Google Play services, so your first scan needs internet. Chinese, Japanese and Korean text recognition downloads the first time you use it.
• Scanning and text recognition use Google ML Kit, which sends Google diagnostic data such as device details and performance.
• Recognised text can have mistakes. Check names and numbers.
• Imported photos are not cropped automatically. To straighten one, tap Scan and pick it from the gallery.
• Scans are not part of Google's cloud backup, so export anything you want to keep elsewhere.
